Dev diary

2024-07-18

The 12th of July marked the end of term, so I started my summer to-do list with the profile page. I estimated about 15 hours to turn my rough ideas into a polished, device-friendly site. It took roughly 45, and mobile layout turned out to be the hardest part. I didn't track every change, so here is a short timeline instead.

Day 0 · 12 July: the about page

I drafted the About page without thinking about mobile, using full-screen sections with about ten words each, which was an odd design choice. I also added favicons. About four hours in, I had my initial ideas laid out.

Day 1 · 13 July: the socials page

I worked through the weekend without committing, because I wanted to finish the Socials page before going live. I borrowed some widget ideas from bento.me, which I had been using as my links page. It worked, but felt clunky to deploy on a single page. About six hours.

Day 2 · 14 July: playlists and debugging

I moved my playlists to their own page instead of crowding the Socials page, and reworked some JavaScript functions and styles. I also stayed up until 5:30 AM to watch Spain win the Euros.

Day 3 · 15 July: mobile layout

I spent an hour or two redesigning the mobile layout. Progress was slow after the late night.

Day 4 · 16 July: colours and positioning

I tidied up directories, made backups, and settled the colour palette and positioning. The site was mostly complete, though the About page still needed work.

Day 5 · 17 July: the About page and TypeScript

I reworked the About page's functionality and design in three to four hours, including a skills tab. Just before publishing, I decided to rewrite the whole index.js in TypeScript to refresh my skills. That took seven hours of rewriting and troubleshooting, and by nearly 11 PM everything worked apart from a few small design issues.

Day 6 · 18 July: bug fixes

I fixed about ten minor bugs and cleaned up the project directory. The design stayed the same. Next I plan to add more blog entries, hopefully at least once a month.
