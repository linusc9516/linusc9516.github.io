Tacit Collusion in LLM Bidders

Oct 2026

Python, pytest · github.com/linusc9516/bid-pricing-collusion

  • Ran a pre-registered no-channel baseline of 48 sessions (2 models, 12 seeds, 50 rounds), each matched with a one-shot control. With history, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash bid about 1 to 2.3 below the Bayes-Nash bid by round 50 and GPT-6 Luna about 6 above it; neither control drifted.
