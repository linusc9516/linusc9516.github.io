Tacit Collusion in LLM Bidders

Oct 2026 linusc9516/bid-pricing-collusion

540 sessions · 3 models · 25 pre-registered tests

  • Built a repeated sealed-bid procurement auction harness and pre-registered the analysis: which single restriction on how LLM bidders communicate removes the most collusion at the least cost? Four arms: no channel, private notes, a shared workspace, an open message channel.
  • Found that open messages, with no instruction to coordinate, produce bid-rotation schemes with punishment threats and raise a collusion index by 0.88, while private notes give no significant rise. One firm is enough: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash makes the first proposal in all 24 mixed sessions, and its rival from another model family sets how far the index rises.
  • Found that one sentence stating a competition authority reviews the messages removes 0.77 of that 0.88 and replicates on new costs and a second model, while a 50-character message cap has no measurable effect.
  • Pre-registered 25 hypotheses in configs/analysis.yaml before their sessions ran, with Holm-corrected paired exact tests and 95% bootstrap intervals over seed pairs; every comparison pairs two sessions on the same seed.
